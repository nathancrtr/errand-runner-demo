# Where I live and what I care about

I live in the Warehouse District in downtown Raleigh, in Council District C. Show me
only what is within my neighborhood, not the whole district; a kilometer or so of wiggle
room is fine. I care about zoning and transit. Noise and parking, much less. If an item
names a street or a case I can't place, look it up before deciding. When nothing near me
is on the agenda, say so in one line and stop.
